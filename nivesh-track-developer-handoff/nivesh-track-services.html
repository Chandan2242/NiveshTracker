Service model

A relationship manager, not just an app.

Every client is assigned one relationship manager at Nivesh Track — a single point of contact across every service line, instead of a rotating support queue.

Goal-based guidance, kept within the rules

For mutual funds specifically, Nivesh Track is permitted to offer what SEBI's framework calls incidental advice — basic, goal-based guidance limited to the schemes we distribute, built on your goal, horizon and risk comfort. You can always choose a scheme yourself; the shortlist from our investment research desk is a starting point, not a requirement.

For PMS, AIF, SIF, insurance, tax and estate planning, your relationship manager's role is to connect you with the specifically registered specialist or partner for that product — not to extend mutual-fund-style guidance into products that sit under a different regulator entirely.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Is Nivesh Track a financial advisor?

No. Nivesh Track, a brand of Cadence Finserve Private Limited, is registered with AMFI as a Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-165724), not as a SEBI Investment Adviser. For mutual funds, your relationship manager can offer basic, goal-based guidance incidental to distribution. For PMS, AIF, SIF, insurance, tax and estate planning, we connect you with the specifically registered specialist for that product.

Do I have to be an existing client to use the app?

No. The app is built to make mutual fund investing smooth for everyone — you can complete KYC and start your first SIP directly from the app.

Can I choose my own mutual fund scheme?

Yes. You can research and select any scheme yourself, or start from a shortlist curated by our investment research desk if you'd like a starting point — either way, the final choice is yours.

What's the difference between a distributor and an RIA?

A distributor (what we are, for mutual funds) earns a commission from the AMC and can offer basic guidance limited to the schemes it distributes. A Registered Investment Adviser charges a fee directly and can give comprehensive, personalised financial planning. We're the former.

What is SIF, and who can invest?

Specialised Investment Funds are SEBI's newer category sitting between mutual funds and PMS, with a ₹10 lakh minimum investment. They're built for investors who want more flexibility than a standard mutual fund but aren't at PMS ticket sizes yet.

What is GIFT City investing?

A route to global markets — US stocks, global ETFs, offshore funds — through GIFT City's International Financial Services Centre, accessed via the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (up to $250,000/year). It's regulated by IFSCA, not SEBI, so we offer it only through a named, IFSCA-registered partner.

Is my insurance or investment amount guaranteed by Nivesh Track?

No. Mutual fund, PMS, AIF and SIF values move with the market and are not guaranteed by Nivesh Track. Insurance cover is underwritten by the insurer on our panel, not by Nivesh Track.

Can I pause or stop a SIP from the app?

SIP modification requests are routed through your relationship manager — reach out through the app's support option or the contact details below.
